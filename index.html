About

I am a chemical engineering Ph.D. candidate focused on advanced process control (APC), process simulation, and optimization for natural gas, LNG, and NGL fractionation systems. As a Graduate Research Assistant at Lamar University, I build steady-state and dynamic simulation models in Aspen Plus and Aspen Dynamics, and implement multivariable control strategies with DMC3 Builder to combine steady-state optimization with real-time control for measurable efficiency gains.

My background also includes hands-on industry experience at a chlor-alkali plant, supporting operations, quality control, and process troubleshooting. I presented my NGL fractionation research at the 2026 AIChE Spring Meeting and completed formal training in process safety and risk analysis.
